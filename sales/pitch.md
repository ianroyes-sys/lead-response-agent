# The Pitch — Lead Response Agent

## The 30-second version (say this out loud)

> "Quick question — when someone fills out your contact form at 8pm on a Tuesday, how long until they hear back from you?
>
> Studies show if you respond within 5 minutes, you're about 9x more likely to win the job than if you wait an hour. But you're on a roof / in a crawlspace / with a patient — you physically can't reply that fast.
>
> I run an AI agent that watches your incoming leads and drafts a personalized reply within a minute — in your voice, referencing exactly what they asked about, with your booking link. You just read it, tap approve, and send. It also queues two follow-ups for the ones who go quiet, which is where most jobs actually get won.
>
> I'll run it free on your real leads for one week. If you don't book at least one extra job from faster responses, we shake hands and part friends. Fair?"

## Why this works

- **It's a known pain, not a pitch.** Every owner has lost a job to "sorry, we already found someone."
- **The free week removes all risk.** Their only cost is forwarding you their leads.
- **"You approve every message"** kills the #1 objection (AI saying something wrong to a customer).

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "AI will sound robotic." | "That's why you approve every draft before it sends. Here — look at these ten." (show demo outbox) |
| "I already reply fast." | "Great — what about at 9pm? Weekends? When you're on a job? The agent never sleeps." |
| "How much?" | "Free for week one. After that $300/month — less than the profit on one job it saves you." |
| "I don't want to change my systems." | "You don't. It reads the Google Sheet / form you already have. Nothing to install." |
| "Is my customer data safe?" | "Leads go to Anthropic's API — same infrastructure Fortune 500s use, not used to train models. And I can show you exactly what's stored: one CSV log you own." |

## Pricing ladder

- **Week 1:** Free pilot on real leads.
- **Starter — $200/mo:** business-hours lead watching, drafts to outbox/Slack.
- **Standard — $350/mo:** 24/7 watching + follow-up sequences + weekly "leads answered" report.
- **Pro — $500/mo:** everything + monthly tuning call + priority setup for new lead sources.

Anchor: "The average HVAC job is worth $400–$6,000. This pays for itself the first time it saves one."

## Who to call first

1. Any owner you personally know (contractor, dentist, realtor, salon).
2. Businesses with a Google Business Profile rating ≥4.2 but **slow review responses** — they care about reputation but lack time.
3. Businesses running Google/Facebook ads — they're literally paying for leads they answer slowly.
